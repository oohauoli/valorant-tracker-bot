Privacy & Authorization

Player data belongs to the player and access is based on explicit authorization.

🔐 Player Authorization

VALORANT Tracker Bot requires players to authenticate through Riot Sign On (RSO) and explicitly authorize the application before accessing their personal VALORANT data.

📋 Data Usage

The application is designed to use authorized player information only for providing the statistics and match history features described on this website.

🚫 What the Bot Does Not Provide

The application does not provide real-time gameplay information, opponent scouting, item store checking, or an alternative MMR/ELO ranking system.
